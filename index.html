FIELD TRAINING
Individual Body Placement
❌ INCORRECT

Body exposed above cover. Enemy can see and hit you.

✅ CORRECT

Only aim out from the side of cover. Minimize exposure.

FIELD TRAINING
Combat Formation
❌ CLUSTERED

Bunching up makes the squad a single grenade/mortar target.

✅ SPREAD LINE

Maintain spacing in a line. Leader in center. Each soldier covers a sector.

FIELD TRAINING
Material Reflection & Camouflage
WITHOUT COVER — AVOID

Bare helmet creates a shine that makes you visible to enemies at range.

WITH COVER — REQUIRED

Helmet cover eliminates shine. Always wear it in the field.

VEHICLE IDENTIFICATION
T-80 Oplot — Main Battle Tank
ROLE: DEFENSIVE

The T-80 is a bulky and low-profile tank. It rarely exposes its sides.

KEY FEATURES
  • Prominent periscope on turret front
  • Blue armor panels on vehicle sides
  • Distinctive engine sound
  • Red-orange IRCM flares
  • Side skirts cover the full tracks
TIP

Never stay alone. Never let allies fight alone.

VEHICLE IDENTIFICATION
BTR-82A — Infantry Fighting Vehicle
ROLE: OFFENSIVE

8-wheeled IFV. Lower profile than BTR3E1.

KEY FEATURES
  • Slim turret — no grenade launcher
  • Dual exhaust pipes both sides
  • Main gun has no shield
  • Dark brown, 8 wheels match body
  • 4-door: 2 front, 2 side
IFV ≠ TANK (รถถัง)

IFV = Infantry Fighting Vehicle. Not a tank.

VEHICLE IDENTIFICATION
BTR-3E1 — Infantry Fighting Vehicle
ROLE: DEFENSIVE

8-wheeled IFV with higher profile. Ukrainian BTR3 chassis.

KEY FEATURES
  • Taller turret with grenade launcher
  • Single exhaust pipe (one side)
  • Larger body overall
  • All 8 wheels match camo pattern
  • Ukrainian camo pattern
IFV ≠ TANK (รถถัง)

4-door: 2 front, 2 side. รถถัง ≠ IFV!
